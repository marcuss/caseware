About Caseware 
Caseware is one of Canada's original Fintech companies, having led the global audit and accounting software industry for 
over 30 years. The company has more than 500,000 users across 130 countries, available in 16 different languages. Over 
36,000 accounting and audit professionals list Caseware as a skill on their LinkedIn profiles. 
As a Staff Java Software Developer, you are a professional who combines strong hands-on Java skills with the ability to 
influence across teams and reduce ambiguity. Architectural decisions and standards are shaped through this role, acting as a 
technical leader through design reviews and RFCs, and mentoring senior and junior engineers alike. 
You serve as a core contributor on the platform, with direct impact on the value Caseware delivers to customers and 
distribution partners globally, owning key features and architectural improvements end -to-end, from design through build, 
using modern, leading-edge technology and architecture. This role does not include managing a development team directly, 
since Caseware already has managers for that function. Instead, the focus is on leading projects, driving architectural and 
technical decisions, and collaborating with developers, product managers, and security teams to move projects forward .
